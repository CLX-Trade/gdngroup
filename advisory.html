HOW WE WORK

Our advisory approach.

Every GDN advisory mandate is structured around evidence, independence and institutional standards — designed to be defensible to a board, an investment committee and an external counterparty.

01 — DISCOVERY

Mandate scoping.

Detailed scoping engagement to define the question, the audience for the deliverable, and the constraints. Workplan, timeline and deliverable list confirmed in writing before commencement.

02 — ANALYSIS

Evidence and modelling.

Primary and secondary research, financial modelling, sensitivity analysis and — where appropriate — direct field assessment. Source-attributed inputs throughout. No assumption left unchallenged.

03 — DELIVERY

Recommendation and handover.

Written report and supporting modelling delivered to client standard. Structured walkthrough with the executive sponsor and any relevant counterparty. Follow-on support priced separately by request.
