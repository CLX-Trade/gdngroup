ADVISORY SERVICES

What we do.

01

Market Assessment

Comprehensive market-entry assessment and commodity flow analysis for sovereign and institutional clients entering new trade corridors. Covering regulatory frameworks, counterparty landscape, pricing dynamics, and infrastructure constraints.

02

Investment Feasibility

End-to-end investment feasibility studies for commodity infrastructure, agricultural development, and energy projects in emerging market jurisdictions. Financial modelling, risk assessment, and capital structuring recommendations.

03

Governance Advisory

Regulatory and governance advisory for governments establishing commodity trade frameworks, sovereign wealth fund mandates, and national development programmes. Includes trade policy design and institutional capacity building.

04

Trade Structure Design

Design and optimisation of trade structures for cross-border commodity transactions, including payment instrument selection, documentation frameworks, and settlement architecture for sovereign and institutional buyers.
