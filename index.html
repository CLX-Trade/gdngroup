ABOUT GDN

Built on expertise. Grounded in markets.

GDN Group (GDN Enterprise Pty Ltd) is an Australian-headquartered trade, advisory and technology group operating at the intersection of physical commodity markets, sovereign investment advisory, and blockchain-enabled trade infrastructure. With over a decade of experience navigating complex international trade frameworks, we serve governments, sovereign wealth vehicles, corporations and commodity buyers across fifteen global trade corridors.
