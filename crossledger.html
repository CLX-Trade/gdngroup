TRADE CORRIDORS

Three live target corridors.

CrossLedger is built around real trade routes — not theoretical use cases. Each corridor has been selected for the friction it removes and the strength of partner conversations underway through GDN Group's existing trade desk.

AUSTRALIA ⟶ ASIA

Bulk commodity export financing

EN590 diesel, ICUMSA 45 sugar, iron ore and LNG flows from Australian ports to South Korea, Japan, and Vietnam. Mid-tier exporters currently absorb 1.5–2.5% in trade-finance costs the platform compresses to under 0.5%.

EAST AFRICA ⟶ EU

Specialty agricultural exports

Ethiopian coffee, Kenyan tea, Tanzanian cashews, and halal poultry to European processors. Smallholder cooperatives currently wait 30+ days for payment; smart escrow targets payment within 24 hours of delivery.

SOUTH AMERICA ⟶ ASIA

Energy and metals logistics

Lithium concentrate, copper cathode, bioethanol and ICUMSA 45 sugar from Chile, Peru, and Brazil into Asia-Pacific processors. Oracle-verified milestone settlement particularly valuable given pricing volatility.
