THE PROBLEM

Global commodity trade is broken.

Cross-border commodity transactions still rely on paper-based letters of credit, manual document verification, and opaque settlement processes that take days or weeks — creating risk, cost and counterparty friction for every participant in the trade chain.

  • Fraud risk from unverified trade documentation
  • Slow, expensive letter of credit settlement
  • No real-time supply chain visibility
  • High counterparty risk for buyers and sellers
THE CROSSLEDGER SOLUTION

Blockchain-verified trade infrastructure.

CrossLedger anchors every trade milestone — from SGS inspection to vessel departure — on Ethereum mainnet. Smart escrow releases funds automatically when conditions are met. No intermediaries. No delays. Full auditability.

  • On-chain verification of SGS certificates, bills of lading, and trade documents
  • Smart escrow: funds released automatically on condition fulfilment
  • Real-time supply chain visibility for all trade participants
  • CLXT token unlocks platform tiers, fee discounts and trade credit
